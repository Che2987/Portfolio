A bit more about me

Chethna is a Product Designer based in Bangalore, currently working within Deloitte's consulting practice.

Her work spans client and internal-tool projects, from a Fortune 500 booking app built for a competitive RFP to an internal insights platform used by Deloitte's own leadership team; usually the kind of work that involves picking up a problem mid-flight as much as starting it from scratch.

Outside of design, she picks up a new skill every few weeks, prefers the beach over the mountains, and spends the rest of her time reading, playing board games, or in the water.
